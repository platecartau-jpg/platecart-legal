PlateCart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 (closed beta)

Short version: we collect only what we need to run PlateCart. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show targeted ads, and you can delete your account and data yourself at any time.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

PlateCart (“we”, “us”) is a recipe, supermarket price comparison (Coles, Woolworths, ALDI) and shopping list app run by an independent developer in Australia. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you use the PlateCart app and website. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Contact: platecartau@gmail.com, or “Me” → “Contact us” in the app.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, name (if provided by Google sign-in) and account ID.
  • Your content: recipes you create or import, favourites, categories, meal plans, shopping lists, notes, ratings, photos and settings.
  • Recipe imports: the post link, caption text or screenshots you give us so we can read the recipe.
  • Location you choose: postcode, suburb, state and favourite store branches.
  • Food preferences: dietary choices you select (for example vegetarian, gluten-free, halal, nut-free).
  • Contact and support: your name (optional), email and message, plus app version, operating system and language.
  • Payments (when paid plans start): payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive only your subscription status, never your card details.

Information collected automatically

  • Device ID: a random ID used to count daily usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • Anonymous usage data, only if you agree (see section 6).
  • Crash reports: error details, device model and OS version, with no recipe content, name or email.

Sensitive information

Dietary choices such as halal, vegetarian or allergy-related settings may reveal health information or religious beliefs, which the Privacy Act treats as sensitive. We collect these only because you choose them, we use them only to filter recipes and labels for you, and we never use them for advertising or share them with third parties.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: sync your content, compare prices, build shopping lists and meal plans.
  • To show prices and specials for your state and chosen stores.
  • To read recipes you import and labels you scan, using AI services.
  • To manage your account and any Pro membership.
  • To answer your messages and fix problems.
  • To keep the service secure, enforce usage limits and prevent misuse.
  • To improve the app using anonymous, aggregated statistics (only with your consent).

We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising, and we only send marketing emails if you ask for them.

4. Location

  • The app asks for location permission only when you tap “Use my location”, never on launch.
  • Exact GPS coordinates are used once to work out your postcode and state, then discarded. They are not stored, uploaded or shared.
  • Your postcode, suburb, state and stores are kept on your device and in your account. They are never shown to other users or given to advertisers.
  • You can use the app without a location (national reference prices are shown) and remove it any time in Settings → My location.

5. Recipes imported from social media

Recipes imported from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and similar platforms are private to you. They are never added to a public library or shown to other users. To read a public post we may fetch its caption, cover image and, if needed, audio, through our service providers. The cover image is copied to private storage that only you can view; deleting the recipe deletes the image. Friends can only see the original post link and your own rating and photos.

6. Anonymous usage data (opt-in)

On first launch the app asks whether you want to “Help improve PlateCart”. Nothing is recorded unless you agree. If you agree, we record actions such as searches, items added or swapped (ingredient type, store, brand, price) and the ingredient types of imported recipes, with a random ID and your state. We do not record your name, email, account ID, exact location or the content of private recipes, and these records are not linked to your account. Reports only show groups of at least 10 people. You can turn this off any time in Settings → Privacy.

Amazon link counts. When you tap an Amazon affiliate link (marked “Affiliate link” in the app), we add one to an anonymous counter that stores only the time and which screen the link was on. It does not include your account, device ID, location or what you searched for, so it cannot be linked to you. This count is separate from the opt-in above and helps us understand how the affiliate links are used.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device in your account, together with your language, your notification choices (half-price alerts and shopping reminders, both on by default), your state (used only to avoid sending notifications between 9pm and 8am your time) and when you last opened the app (used to decide whether to send a reminder). We send at most 3 notifications a week. We also count how many notifications were sent and opened, as totals only. You can turn each type off in Settings, or turn notifications off in your phone settings.

Free Pro trial. To give each person one free 7-day trial, the server records the trial start and end dates in your account, a count of how many times you used Pro features during the trial (such as recipe imports and price searches, shown to you in a reminder when it ends), and a random device ID in a separate list that is not linked to your account and is kept after an account is deleted, so the same phone cannot start a new trial. If you allow notifications, we send two one-off trial reminders (2 days left, and trial ended), which are not counted in the 3 a week above. If you tap “Notify me” for a paid plan, we store only the plan and your app language, with no account or device ID.

8. Overseas storage and transfers

Some of our providers store or process information outside Australia, mainly in the United States and the European Union. We choose reputable providers, send them only what they need, and require them to protect it.

9. How long we keep information

  • Account content: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • “Contact us” messages: kept to resolve issues; your name and email are removed if you delete your account.
  • Daily usage counters: a short time only.
  • Crash reports: as set by Sentry’s retention, usually up to 90 days.
  • Price and specials history is public product data, not personal information.

10. Deleting your account and data

When signed in, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, recipes, lists, meal plans, settings, location and private photos. It cannot be undone. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

11. Your rights

  • Access and correction: ask us for a copy of your personal information or to correct it. We aim to reply within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off usage data, location or dietary settings at any time.
  • Anonymity: you can browse recipes and prices without an account.
  • Complaints: email us first and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

12. Security and data breaches

Data is sent over encrypted connections, database rules limit each user to their own content, and service keys stay on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected users and the OAIC as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

13. Children

PlateCart is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores your settings and offline data on your device so it works without a connection.

15. Changes to this policy

We will update the date above when we change this policy. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app before they take effect.
