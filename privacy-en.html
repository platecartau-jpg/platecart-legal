PlateCart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 (closed beta)

PlateCart is in closed beta. This policy explains how we handle your data and will be updated as the app changes.

1. Who we are

PlateCart is a recipe, supermarket price comparison (Coles, Woolworths, ALDI) and shopping list app run by an independent developer. For any privacy question, email platecartau@gmail.com or use “Me” → “Contact us” in the app.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and account ID when you sign in with Google or email.
  • Your content: recipes you create or import, favourites, meal plan, shopping lists, settings and photos you upload. When signed in, these sync to your own account; database rules allow only you to read and write them.
  • What you provide when importing a recipe: the link, caption text or screenshots you paste.
  • Location data: the postcode, suburb and state you type, and the stores you choose as favourites.
  • Contact us: your message, reply email, plus app version, operating system and language.
  • Anonymous usage data (only with your consent): see section 5.
  • Device ID: used to count daily usage and prevent abuse.

3. Location

  • The app asks for location permission only when you tap “Use my location”, never on launch.
  • Exact GPS coordinates are used only to work out your postcode and state on the spot, then discarded. They are not stored, uploaded or shared.
  • Your postcode, suburb, state and favourite stores stay in your own device and account. They do not appear in friend activity, shared recipes or any other feature, and are never sold or given to advertisers.
  • You can use the app without sharing a location (prices fall back to national reference prices). You can remove it any time in Settings → My location.

4. Privately imported recipes

Recipes imported from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and similar are private to you. They are not public and not shown to other users. You can only share the original post link and your own rating and photos, never the full content. The post’s cover image is copied into private storage that only you can view through a signed link valid for 1 hour; deleting the recipe deletes the cover image.

5. Anonymous usage data

On first launch the app asks whether you want to “Help improve PlateCart”. Nothing is recorded unless you agree. If you do, we record: search terms; ingredient type or product, store, brand and price when items are added, swapped or removed; the ingredient types (only) of imported recipes; official recipe IDs viewed; plus a random ID and your state. We do not record your name, email, account ID or exact location, nor the title, steps, link or photos of private recipes, and this data is not linked to your account. Reports only show groups with at least 10 distinct anonymous IDs. You can turn this off any time in Settings → Privacy, which deletes the random ID and unsent records from your phone. Because sent records are not linked to you, individual records cannot be picked out and deleted afterwards.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Account content is kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • You can delete individual recipes, lists and your location inside the app. To delete your whole account and related data, contact us in the app or by email and we will delete your recipes, lists, photos and location. “Contact us” messages may be kept to resolve your issue, but you can ask us to remove your email address from them.
  • Public price data such as price history is not personal data.

8. User content, reporting and blocking

Wherever there is user content (for example reviews and photos), you can report inappropriate content and block other users. We review reports and may remove content or disable accounts that break the rules.

9. Children

PlateCart is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email platecartau@gmail.com; we will reply within a reasonable time. If you are in Australia you may also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and row-level database rules limit access to your own content. AI service keys are kept on the server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure.

12. Changes

If we make significant changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
