What PlateCart does

  • Recipes with pricesHome-cooked favourites from Australian, Asian, Mediterranean, Mexican, Indian and Western kitchens. Every recipe lists its ingredients with prices from the three big supermarkets.
  • Coles · Woolworths · ALDICompare unit prices side by side, see this week's half-price specials, and see when each price was last updated.
  • One shopping listAdd a whole recipe or single items. Items are grouped by store and aisle, and you can tick them off in the shop.
  • Same product, other storeThe app matches products across stores (by barcode when available) and tells you when pack sizes differ.
  • Import recipes from linksPaste an Instagram or Facebook recipe link. The imported recipe is private to your account.
  • Cooking helpersStep-by-step instructions with timers, nutrition information, and ingredient swaps.
  • 10 languagesEnglish, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi and Arabic.
  • Privacy firstYour location stays in your own account. Anonymous usage statistics are optional. You can delete your account in the app.
