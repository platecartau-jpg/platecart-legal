Delete your PlateCart account

App: PlateCart (com.platecart.app) · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open PlateCart and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The deletion happens straight away.

What is deleted

Your account, your recipes (including imported recipes and their private images), shopping lists, meal plans, favourites, ratings, photos, settings, saved location (postcode, state, preferred store), and notification token. Your name and email are removed from any “Contact us” messages you sent. This cannot be undone.

What may be kept

Anonymous totals that cannot be linked to you (for example, how many notifications were sent, or how many times Amazon links were tapped) are kept.

Delete some of your data (keep your account)

  • A recipe (including an imported recipe and its private image): open the recipe → ⋯ → Delete.
  • Shopping list items: Shopping list → swipe or tap the bin icon, or “Clear bought”.
  • Meal plan, favourites and saved products: tap the item again to remove it.
  • Saved location (postcode, state, store): Settings → My location → Remove my location.
  • Anonymous usage data: Settings → Privacy → turn off “Help improve PlateCart”.

Deleted items are removed from your account straight away. You can also email platecartau@gmail.com to ask us to delete specific data; we will do it within 30 days.

Can’t open the app?

Email platecartau@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.
